**What happened:** The article discusses the proactive steps hardware manufacturers are taking by integrating post-quantum cryptography into their systems. As quantum computing capabilities advance, traditional encryption methods become increasingly vulnerable to being compromised. By adopting post-quantum cryptography measures now, these companies aim to safeguard data against future cyber threats facilitated by quantum technology.

**Why it matters:** The implementation of post-quantum cryptography is crucial because quantum computers have the potential to break widely-used encryption algorithms, such as RSA and ECC, which underpin vast portions of today’s digital communication and data protection mechanisms. Without preparation, sensitive data and informational infrastructure would be at severe risk, threatening personal, business, and national security.

**What actions should be taken:** Stakeholders in information security, especially those in sectors handling sensitive data, should begin evaluating and transitioning to post-quantum cryptography frameworks. It is important to start with an assessment of current cryptographic practices and to educate the relevant teams about upcoming changes and challenges. Collaboration with hardware and software vendors is essential to ensure a seamless integration of new cryptographic standards. Additionally, staying informed about developments in quantum computing will enable timely adjustments to strategies.

Original Article: https://www.darkreading.com/application-security/owasp-flags-top-ai-skill-risks-security-blueprint

1) According to the article's title, the Open Web Application Security Project (OWASP) has identified the top risks associated with skills in artificial intelligence (AI) as part of a new security blueprint. This suggests that OWASP is focusing on the skills required to manage and secure AI technologies effectively. The inability to access the content details implies that the risks might involve lack of expertise or oversight in deploying AI solutions securely.

2) This development is significant because AI is increasingly integrated into various applications and systems, contributing to operational efficiency and new capabilities. However, without adequate skills and awareness of potential risks, AI implementations can introduce vulnerabilities, expose sensitive data, and lead to misuse or biases in decision-making processes. Addressing these risks is crucial to ensure that AI systems are both reliable and secure, preserving trust in these technologies.

3) Organizations should consider evaluating their current AI systems and skill sets to identify potential gaps in expertise or understanding that could lead to security vulnerabilities. Investing in training programs and raising awareness about AI-specific threats can help in building a knowledgeable workforce capable of managing AI risks effectively. Additionally, employing guidelines like those proposed by OWASP can help in forming stringent policies and controls around AI system development and deployment, reducing the likelihood of security breaches.

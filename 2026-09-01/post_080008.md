Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/recently-patched-papercut-zero-days-used-in-data-theft-attacks/

Two recently discovered security vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078, in the PaperCut NG and MF print management software were actively exploited as zero-days. This exploitation enabled attackers to bypass authentication and execute remote code, leading to data theft attacks. Although emergency patches have been issued, attackers have leveraged these vulnerabilities to access and steal database information from compromised servers, as confirmed by the observed exploit activity on honeypots.

This situation is critical as PaperCut software is widely used by numerous organizations, including government bodies and educational institutions, which could potentially lead to significant data breaches. Previous instances have seen similar vulnerabilities targeted by state-backed groups and ransomware gangs, highlighting the importance of securing public-facing applications. This ongoing threat poses risks not only for data confidentiality but also for operational integrity if attackers gain broader access to systems.

Organizations using PaperCut software should prioritize applying the emergency patches and verify their systems against the published indicators of compromise to identify any breaches. Additionally, enhancing their monitoring for abnormal activities and restricting server exposure could mitigate further risks. Conducting immediate security audits and reviewing access controls could provide additional layers of defense, reducing potential exploitation pathways.

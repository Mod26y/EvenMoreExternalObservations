1) OpenAI has started the gradual rollout of ChatGPT Astra, its most advanced model thus far, for users with a $20 Plus subscription. Astra is accessible through ChatGPT Work and Codex, with plans for broader availability in professional and business settings. The model provides superior capabilities for complex tasks encompassing coding, cybersecurity, and science, while better retaining context compared to previous iterations. While Astra is currently not available to free users, those subscribed to ChatGPT Plus do not require additional purchases, though extra usage can be supplemented by buying credits.

The gradual rollout strategy also highlights OpenAI’s focus on reliability and user experience. However, the exclusion of free-tier users might widen accessibility gaps in utilizing leading AI technologies, underscoring ongoing debates around equitable access to advanced digital tools.

Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-protection-for-msps-a-6-point-checklist-for-faster-recovery/

**1) What happened:**
The article outlines a six-point checklist aimed at Managed Service Providers (MSPs) for enhancing ransomware protection and expediting recovery. It highlights key aspects such as reducing exposure, detecting threats before encryption, ensuring 24/7 incident response, preserving isolated recovery points, recovering cleanly, and operating consistently across tenants. The context derives from the Acronis Cyberthreats Report, revealing 143 MSP-related ransomware incidents in 2025, mainly due to phishing and unpatched vulnerabilities. The article promotes Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud, emphasizing the need for a comprehensive approach combining prevention, detection, response, and recovery, rather than relying solely on backups or endpoint detection.

**2) Why it matters:**
Ransomware attacks pose significant threats to MSPs, who are responsible for safeguarding data across multiple client environments. The checklist emphasizes the vital need for proactive measures and a holistic approach to ransomware protection. MSPs are primary targets due to their access to multiple client networks, making them lucrative points of entry for cybercriminals. A failure to adequately protect against ransomware can result in significant financial losses, reputational damage, and legal liabilities. By understanding the full scope of these threats, MSPs can better ensure business continuity and data protection for their clients.

**3) What actions should be taken:**
MSPs should implement a comprehensive ransomware protection strategy aligned with the provided checklist. This includes setting patch service level agreements (SLAs), enforcing multi-factor authentication (MFA), conducting regular behavioral tests, confirming a 24/7 response system, and maintaining immutable and offline backup copies. Additionally, they should regularly verify role separation and cross-tenant visibility. It is crucial for MSPs to conduct regular rehearsals for incident response plans, automate routine processes to minimize delay, and ensure that their chosen platform can consistently achieve desired recovery time objectives. Continuous assessment and validation are key to understanding and mitigating risks efficiently.

Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/rogue-ransomware-affiliate-ransom-busters-poses-as-recovery-firm/

The article describes a rogue ransomware group posing as a recovery service, Ransom Busters, that contacts ransomware victims before attacks become public. They claim to access decryption keys and offer to delete stolen data for a fee. Investigations by GuidePoint Security's GRIT suggest that Ransom Busters is likely a ransomware affiliate using these tactics to divert payments from actual ransomware operators, compromising victims further by potentially not providing true recovery services.

This situation is significant as it highlights an increase in sophisticated attacks where affiliates leverage insider knowledge to exploit victims more effectively. Such activities introduce additional risks, like re-victimization and loss of trust in ransomware recovery services. Victims might end up paying for services that provide no real benefit, and even if they pay ransom demands, their data might still be endangered by unauthorized parties.

Organizations should enhance their due diligence when selecting recovery services and consider working with reputable IR firms. It's crucial to establish a robust cybersecurity posture, including regular security audits and immediate incident response protocols. Strengthening access management to detect and respond to unauthorized credential use can also help mitigate potential breaches and limits adversary action post-compromise.

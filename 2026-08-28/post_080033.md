Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/manchester-airports-group-says-hackers-stole-travelers-data/

The Manchester Airports Group (MAG) experienced a cyberattack where hackers accessed customer data associated with Wi-Fi sign-ups and service bookings at Manchester, Stansted, and East Midlands airports. The compromised information includes email addresses, phone numbers, vehicle registration numbers, and postcodes, though customer payment details were unaffected. MAG promptly contained the breach, involved external experts, and notified law enforcement. The online “Manage My Booking” service was temporarily suspended to mitigate further risk.

This incident highlights significant cybersecurity vulnerabilities even at well-established entities like MAG, underscoring the critical nature of protecting personal data. The potential exposure of up to 8.9 million travelers' data accentuates the risk of identity theft and phishing attacks, and disrupts consumer trust. This breach serves as a reminder for organizations to prioritize and continuously update their cybersecurity defenses, ensuring robust protection for customer data.

As a proactive measure, MAG advised affected customers to be wary of suspicious communications and emphasized the importance of recognizing phishing attempts. Organizations should enhance employee training on security protocols, improve monitoring systems to detect unauthorized activities quickly, and adhere to cybersecurity frameworks like those from the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) for best practices in incident response and prevention. A thorough review and upgrading of legacy systems could also prevent similar cyber intrusions.

Original Article: https://www.darkreading.com/cyber-risk/stronger-security-drives-ransomware-groups-to-recruit-from-within

**What happened:** Due to advancements in cybersecurity measures, ransomware groups are increasingly trying to bypass external barriers by recruiting insiders within target organizations. This recruitment approach allows attackers to circumvent strengthened security systems by exploiting internal access. Insiders can provide critical assistance in launching attacks by sharing sensitive information or directly deploying malware from within the network.

**Why it matters:** The shift towards insider recruitment emphasizes the evolving methods threat actors use in response to heightened security defenses. This trend highlights the persistent risk of insider threats, complicating cybersecurity efforts as it leverages trusted access to sidestep perimeter protections. Organizations need to recognize that not all threats are external and adjust their security strategies to encompass internal vulnerabilities and personnel-related risks.

**What actions should be taken:** To counter this emerging threat, organizations may consider enhancing their insider threat programs by incorporating robust monitoring systems to detect anomalous activities and behavior changes. Regular training and awareness programs can be implemented to promote a culture of security and vigilance among employees. Additionally, companies can review and strengthen access control measures, ensuring that only those with a legitimate need have access to sensitive areas of the network. Regular audits and background checks should also be part of a comprehensive defense strategy.

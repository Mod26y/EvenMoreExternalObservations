In this incident, hackers executed a BGP hijacking attack to misdirect update requests from Virtualizor VPS management software to their own malicious servers, allowing them to distribute corrupted updates. The attack impacted a select number of installations and was facilitated by rerouted IP address traffic from the Softaculous update infrastructure. As legitimate routing announcements were hijacked, some traffic was deceptively diverted to the attackers’ infrastructure, permitting modified or redirected updates delivery to unsuspecting users.

This attack underscores the vulnerabilities present in the internet's routing infrastructure, like the BGP, and the potential for sophisticated threats that result from hijacking these routes. Malicious updates can lead to unauthorized access to servers, expose sensitive information, and disrupt operations. Ensuring the integrity of software updates is crucial for maintaining the reliability and security of IT systems, especially for hosting providers relying on such control panels to perform essential functions.

In response to this information, organizations using Virtualizor should inspect their systems for signs of compromise, specifically looking for the unauthorized `java-jre-update.service`. Administrators should change and restrict API keys, audit systems for unrecognized SSH keys and accounts, and scrutinize any abnormal activities and connections. Users who accessed sensitive areas of Softaculous during the attack window are advised to change their passwords, check their account activities, and keep an eye on their financial statements for any irregularities. Adopting cryptographic signing for updates and deploying comprehensive infrastructure improvements are also recommended to mitigate future risks.

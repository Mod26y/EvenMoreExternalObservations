Original Article: https://www.darkreading.com/cybersecurity-operations/patch-gap-defenders-chains-not-checklists

**What happened:**  
The article discusses the concept of the "patch gap" where vulnerabilities remain exposed despite available patches. This gap arises because organizations either delay applying patches or follow overly simplistic checklist approaches that fail to consider interconnected system weaknesses. Attackers exploit these gaps as they understand systems in a holistic, chain-like manner. Thus, defenders need to rethink their strategies beyond isolated patching, focusing on how different components interact and affect overall security posture.

**Why it matters:**  
Addressing the patch gap is critical as it exposes organizations to potential cyber-attacks even when they believe they are secure. This gap showcases a misalignment between rapid threat evolution and slower defensive responses, emphasizing the need for a more integrated security strategy. Organizations face increasing risks not just from known vulnerabilities but also from the dependencies that arise within their IT ecosystems. Bridging this gap is essential for enhancing resilience and reducing the attack surface.

**What actions should be taken:**  
Organizations should develop a more comprehensive approach to vulnerability management by considering the interaction between systems and potential chain-reaction impacts of vulnerabilities. This includes implementing automated and continuous monitoring tools that provide visibility into the interdependencies and prioritize patching based on risk assessments. Additionally, security teams could benefit from broader threat modeling practices, aligning their patch management with a holistic understanding of system architecture, thereby closing the security gaps that attackers might exploit.

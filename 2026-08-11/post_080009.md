Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-breached-a-small-polish-energy-plant-via-private-apn-last-year/

**1) What happened:**

Hackers allegedly linked to the Russian Electrum threat group breached a small Polish combined heat-and-power plant via a misconfigured private Access Point Name (APN) in December 2025. This breach allowed them to disable key components of the plant, including a steam turbine and water treatment system, by exploiting a FortiGate VPN/firewall and a Teltonika cellular router. The hackers entered through a WAGO PFC200 PLC with default credentials, moved laterally within the network, and disrupted operations using a novel attack vector involving the APN. 

**2) Why it matters:**

This incident highlights a novel cybersecurity threat vector whereby attackers leverage private APNs with misconfigured settings to infiltrate operational technology (OT) networks. Given that similar configurations are common globally, this attack underscores vulnerabilities in network management within critical infrastructure sectors. Such breaches could lead to severe disruptions in essential services, extending beyond immediate financial losses to potentially impacting public safety and national security. The incident also illustrates the risk posed by default credentials and inadequate network segmentation in protecting critical infrastructure.

**3) What actions should be taken:**

Organizations managing critical infrastructure should treat private APNs as potentially hostile entities, ensuring proper client isolation and implementing stringent access controls. It is advisable to conduct comprehensive security audits to eliminate default credentials and misconfigurations. Deploying allowlists for traffic between APN gateways and OT systems can restrict unauthorized communication. Disabling exposed administration services such as SSH and Telnet can curb unauthorized access. Learning from this incident, regular penetration testing and breach simulations should be performed to assess and enhance readiness against evolving cyber threats.

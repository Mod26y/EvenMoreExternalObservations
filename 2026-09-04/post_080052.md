Original Article: https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/ai-machine-speed-2-week-attack-10-hours

**What happened:** The article discusses a significant advancement in cyberattack capabilities facilitated by artificial intelligence (AI). AI technologies have markedly accelerated the speed of executing cyberattacks, reducing the time needed from weeks to mere hours. Previously, cybercriminal activities that took approximately two weeks can now be completed in just 10 hours. This demonstrates AI's potential to automate and expedite the phases of a cyberattack, from reconnaissance and exploitation to extraction, posing a new challenge to conventional cybersecurity defenses.

**Why it matters:** This development is critical because it represents a paradigm shift in the threat landscape. With attacks occurring at 'machine speed,' cybersecurity defenses that rely on human intervention or slow detection and response mechanisms could be easily overwhelmed. This enhancement of attack speed increases the likelihood of successful breaches before organizations can react or deploy countermeasures. It underscores the urgent need for improved detection and response systems that are also capable of operating at machine speed to effectively counter these AI-driven cyber threats.

**What actions should be taken:** Organizations should consider investing in AI-driven cybersecurity solutions to match the speed of potential threats. Enhanced machine-learning-based detection systems could detect anomalies or malicious activities almost in real-time, providing faster incident response. Regularly updating and patching systems remain crucial to mitigate vulnerabilities. Additionally, training and awareness programs for cybersecurity staff about AI developments in cyber threats are important to ensure preparedness and adaptability to the evolving threat landscape. Exploring partnerships with cybersecurity vendors specializing in AI and machine learning technologies might also offer additional resilience against these rapid attacks.

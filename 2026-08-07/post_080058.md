This gap can involve inadequacies in communication and collaboration across jurisdictions and agencies, allowing attackers to exploit these weaknesses to execute more sophisticated and swift cyberattacks. As attackers adopt advanced tactics and share information swiftly, law enforcement struggles to keep pace in terms of technological capabilities and cross-border cooperation.

3) To address these challenges, increasing cooperation among international law enforcement agencies and optimizing communication channels is crucial.

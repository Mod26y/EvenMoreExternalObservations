Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-critical-adobe-commerce-flaw-to-hijack-customer-accounts/

The article details a critical vulnerability (CVE-2026-71362) in Adobe's Commerce and Magento platforms, allowing attackers to hijack customer accounts without requiring existing accounts or user interaction. This incorrect authorization flaw permits attackers to switch customer sessions, accessing sensitive personal data. Despite Adobe's recent patches, exploitation attempts have been identified, necessitating vigilant security measures from eCommerce platforms.

This vulnerability is significant as it poses a substantial risk to customer privacy and security, potentially leading to data breaches and financial loss. With attackers potentially accessing private customer data, the integrity of impacted e-commerce platforms is threatened, underscoring the critical nature of applying security updates promptly. The exploitation of such vulnerabilities can damage company reputations, erode customer trust, and incur regulatory penalties for inadequate data protection.

In response, administrators should prioritize applying the latest Adobe Commerce security updates, particularly the isolated patch files for their software version. Regularly updating security measures, employing web application firewalls like Sansec’s Shield, and conducting routine cybersecurity audits are critical. Additionally, enhancing monitoring capabilities to detect unauthorized session switches can help mitigate potential breaches and safeguard customer data against unauthorized access.

**1) What happened:**  
Anthropic is adjusting the weekly usage limits for its Claude Code service. Presently, users are enjoying a temporary 50% increase in weekly usage, which will last until September 13. Starting September 14, these limits will permanently increase by 25% compared to their original standard limits. However, this still represents a 17% reduction compared to the current temporary boost. Anthropic initially framed this as a beneficial change, but later clarified that it entails a cutback from current levels. Additionally, Anthropic teased further developments aimed at improving service elements like visibility and control.

**2) Why it matters:**  
These changes are noteworthy as they impact user expectations and resource management for those reliant on Anthropic's Claude Code services, particularly within institutional frameworks that utilize artificial intelligence (AI). The reduction in access could affect productivity or project timelines that depend on extensive AI support.

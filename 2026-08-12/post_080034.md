Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-warns-of-asa-and-ftd-vpn-flaw-exploited-to-crash-devices/

Cisco disclosed a high-severity vulnerability, CVE-2026-20349, affecting its Secure Firewall ASA and FTD software, which is actively exploited to crash devices remotely. This denial-of-service flaw, initiated by crafted HTTP requests, can be exploited without authentication when specific VPN services are enabled. Impacted configurations include IKEv2 Remote Access VPN, SSL VPN, and Zero Trust Network Access. Cisco revealed no workarounds and urged users to apply available hotfixes for affected software versions to prevent exploitation.

This vulnerability matters because it can be used to disrupt business operations by crashing critical network security devices, leading to potential breaches and loss of service support. The remote, unauthenticated nature of this flaw magnifies the threat, leading to increased risk of attacks targeting vital VPN services, which are often crucial for remote work and secure connections.

Organizations should promptly identify any ASA or FTD devices in their fleet and assess whether they are running susceptible configurations. Upgrading to fixed software releases is critical to avert exploitation. Regularly monitoring firewall logs for unusual HTTP traffic and maintaining updated network defense strategies offer additional protection layers while awaiting further vulnerability indicators from Cisco.

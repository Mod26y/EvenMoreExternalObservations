1) GhostJacking is a new cyber threat involving the hijacking of AI agents to manipulate identity governance systems. This exploit leverages AI systems' vulnerabilities to compromise user identities and impersonate legitimate operations in an organization's workflow. It highlights significant gaps in the security infrastructures governing AI agents, leading to potential unauthorized access or data manipulation, which could be detrimental to sensitive operations or personal data safety.

2) This issue is vital because AI agents play an increasingly prominent role in automating and optimizing business processes. As organizations integrate AI into their systems, understanding and mitigating security risks associated with these agents becomes crucial. The exploitation of identity governance gaps in AI systems could lead to widespread data breaches, affecting consumer trust and causing potential legal repercussions. Furthermore, as AI becomes more entrenched, its security will directly impact organization's overall cybersecurity resilience.

3) To address the potential risks of GhostJacking, organizations can conduct comprehensive security audits of their AI systems, focusing on identity governance and authentication processes. Implementing stronger access controls and monitoring mechanisms will help in detecting unauthorized activities swiftly. Additionally, employee awareness training should be updated to include new threats posed by AI systems, ensuring that staff are informed of the latest vulnerabilities. Developing a continuous review process for AI-driven operations will help in recognizing and mitigating emerging cyber threats effectively.

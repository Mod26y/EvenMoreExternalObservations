Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/coldcard-wallet-rng-flaw-likely-linked-to-88-million-bitcoin-theft/

1) The incident involves a significant vulnerability within COLDCARD hardware wallets, allowing an attacker to exploit the flawed random number generator (RNG) to steal an estimated $88.6 million in Bitcoin. The exploitation was achieved by generating predictable wallet seeds offline due to an integration error, and confirmations of the correct seeds enabled access to them. The attacker conducted a meticulously planned heist, impacting thousands of wallets and targeting high-value cryptocurrencies first. The issue was not fully apparent until significant losses had occurred, and the flawed transactions were logged.

2) This security failure underscores the critical nature of secure random number generation in safeguarding cryptocurrency assets. The incident matters as it highlights vulnerabilities in digital asset devices that, if compromised, can lead directly to extensive financial losses. For organizations and individuals invested in crypto-assets, ensuring secure and tested hardware and firmware is essential. This event serves as a crucial reminder for users to stay vigilant and keep their wallets and devices updated against potential vulnerabilities.

3) In response, stakeholders should immediately update the firmware on affected COLDCARD devices to the latest secure versions and ensure all procedures for generating seeds are followed strictly. Users are advised to generate new wallet seeds, record balances securely, and migrate funds while verifying new addresses. Retaining old backups until confirmation of successful migration is also recommended. Affected parties should adopt ongoing testing and reviews of firmware operations and implement preventive measures like supplementary dice rolls or unique passphrases to enhance protection from prospective vulnerabilities.

Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/microsoft-teams-now-lets-admins-block-external-bots-from-meetings/

Microsoft has introduced a new feature in Teams that allows administrators to automatically block all identified external bots from joining meetings. This policy enhances a June update that tagged external bots for organizer approval, by now preventing them entirely from joining without approval. The feature aims to give organizations more control over meeting security by configuring Teams policies to automatically block these bots, thus reducing potential risks. It is being rolled out as part of a targeted release until August's end, with global availability expected by late September.

Blocking external bots is significant as it enhances security in the face of increasing attacks using Teams as a vector, where attackers impersonate IT staff to gain access and move laterally within enterprise networks. This proactive measure helps in preventing external malicious apps and automated tasks which might otherwise disrupt meetings or pose a data security risk. This not only heightens security but also ensures that team members are aware if any external non-human participant is present during meetings.

Organizations should consider activating this new policy in the Teams admin center, evaluate its implications, and assign it to appropriate users or groups in their Teams meeting policy management. Further, organizations might benefit from educating employees on recognizing bots and utilizing additional controls announced by Microsoft, such as creating allow lists for approved bots and monitoring admin reports for any unauthorized access attempts. This layered security approach will help in safeguarding against potential compromises through social engineering attacks via Teams.

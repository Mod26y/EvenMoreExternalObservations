Original Article: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/coldcard-security-audit-phishing-attack-installs-remote-access-tool/

A phishing campaign has been identified targeting users concerned about a recently discovered vulnerability in COLDCARD wallets, falsely alleging a security audit to install ScreenConnect remote access software. Emails impersonate COLDCARD, urging recipients to initiate a security verification process through a site that mimics the official platform. Installation of a malicious batch file whose payload grants attackers remote control over users' computers is the end goal, facilitating potential data theft or further malicious activities, including ransomware deployment.

This incident highlights the evolving and sophisticated techniques utilized in phishing attacks, particularly exploiting known vulnerabilities and user concerns. The use of real-time chat assistance further exemplifies personalized social engineering tactics. Effective phishing attacks not only compromise data but can also lead to significant financial losses, especially in the context of cryptocurrencies. The event underscores the critical need for organizations to continually educate their users and employ rigorous verification practices.

Users should maintain vigilance by independently validating such notifications directly with service providers and avoid clicking links in unsolicited emails. Security teams could benefit from reinforcing email filtering protocols and conducting regular phishing awareness training. Additionally, analyzing system logs for suspicious activity can help detect and mitigate such threats before they cause substantial harm. Enhanced endpoint protection could prevent unauthorized remote access, thereby safeguarding valuable data.

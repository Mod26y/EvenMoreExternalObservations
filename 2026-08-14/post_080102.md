Purple teaming combines red team (attackers) and blue team (defenders) efforts to identify vulnerabilities and improve defenses in a collaborative environment. Walmart's use of 'Trusted Agents'—presumably key insiders or privileged individuals—suggests an emphasis on fostering trust and cooperation between these groups to fine-tune security measures and simulate real-world attacks while also testing and strengthening response strategies.

2) This approach matters because it represents a progressive shift towards collaboration in cybersecurity operations, signaling the importance of bridging gaps between offensive and defensive teams. By employing Trusted Agents, organizations can take a holistic view of their security posture, better preparing them for actual threats. Trusted Agents can facilitate effective communication, ensuring both proactive and reactive measures are constantly reviewed and improved. This methodology can increase the effectiveness of threat detection, incident response, and enhance overall resilience against cyber attacks.

Appointing or training Trusted Agents—individuals with a deep understanding of both the organization's operations and cybersecurity threats—can also be valuable.
